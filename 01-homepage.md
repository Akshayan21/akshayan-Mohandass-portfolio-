They’ve heard your name. Someone shared your number. They’re comparing a few businesses before making a call.

Your website is where they check the details.

What do you offer? Do you serve their area? Have you done the kind of work they need? How do they get in touch?

## Your product idea needs a journey people can follow.

A feature list can explain what an app contains. It doesn’t explain how someone will use it.

For startup founders, I help turn requirements into user flows, interface designs, and clickable prototypes. We work through the decisions that affect the experience before they become expensive development changes.

That might mean helping a new user get started, making a booking easier to complete, or organising a dashboard around the work someone needs to do.

## Questions about hiring a freelance designer and developer

### Can you design and develop my business website?

Yes. We can scope website structure, design, responsive development, and launch together. Features such as booking, payments, or custom integrations are assessed before I commit to the build.

### Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. A focused engagement can help define one core product journey, create a clickable prototype, or build a landing page for an upcoming launch.

### How much does website design or UI/UX design cost?

The fee depends on the scope and deliverables. Share your budget range and priorities, and I’ll recommend a realistic starting point. You receive a quote before work begins.

### Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes, subject to reviewing the current website, technology, and access available. We can discuss a focused set of improvements or a broader redesign.

### Do you provide website content?

I can help organise your information and refine supplied copy within the agreed scope. More extensive research and copywriting can be included separately.

### Will my website work on mobile?

Responsive layouts are part of the website scope. The agreed pages and interactions are checked across relevant screen sizes before handover.

### Do you provide ongoing website maintenance?

We can arrange ongoing support for agreed updates and improvements. The proposal defines capacity, response expectations, and what is included.

### Will a new website guarantee more enquiries?

No. A website can help visitors understand your business and contact you, but results also depend on your offer, traffic, reputation, and follow-up.
