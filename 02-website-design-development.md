## Your website should answer the questions that come before an enquiry.

Someone referred you. A potential customer searched for your service. A prospect opened the link in your proposal.

They need to know:

- What exactly do you do?
- Do you handle the kind of work they need?
- Where do you provide your services?
- What can they see that supports your claims?
- How do they take the next step?

We’ll build the website around those decisions, using your actual services, experience, and way of working.

## Website design and development for small businesses

### Put your services into words people understand.

Business owners know their work so well that important details can go unstated.

We’ll organise the information customers need: what you offer, who it suits, what is included, and how an engagement begins. Content organisation and light editing can form part of the scope; full copywriting is agreed separately.

### Give visitors evidence they can assess.

A useful project example, a clearly explained process, or a genuine customer testimonial can answer questions that a broad claim cannot.

We’ll identify the proof you already have and give it an appropriate place. Nothing gets invented to fill a section.

### Make the website comfortable to use on a phone.

Your customer may open the link between meetings or while speaking to someone about your business.

The agreed pages will use responsive layouts, readable text, and contact options that work across relevant screen sizes.

### Build a clear route to contacting you.

The right next step depends on your business. It could be a call, a WhatsApp conversation, an email, or a form asking for project details.

We’ll choose the route deliberately and make it easy to find.

## Starting fresh or replacing what you have?

### A new business website

You need a credible place to send people beyond a social profile or a phone number.

We’ll establish the essential pages and make your offer understandable before deciding what else the site needs.

### An existing website that no longer fits

Your services have changed. The content is out of date. The site looks acceptable but leaves customers with unanswered questions.

We’ll review what is worth keeping, what needs rewriting, and whether targeted improvements or a rebuild makes more sense.

### A website with a specific problem

Perhaps the service pages are unclear, the mobile experience is awkward, or the contact journey needs work.

We can scope a defined set of changes without assuming the whole website needs replacing.

## Questions about your business website

### Do you work with businesses anywhere in India?

Yes. We can collaborate remotely through scheduled calls, shared documents, and agreed review points. Any requirement for in-person work is discussed separately.

### What do I need before we begin?

Your business details, service information, existing brand assets, and any photographs or examples you want to use. You do not need everything perfectly organised; we’ll identify the gaps during planning.

### Can you help write the website content?

I can organise your information and refine supplied text within the agreed scope. Research, interviews, and writing the full website from scratch can be scoped separately.

### Will I be able to update the website myself?

If regular self-managed updates are important, tell me during the brief. We’ll choose an appropriate content-management approach and include the relevant handover.

### Is SEO included?

The website scope can include on-page foundations such as descriptive titles, headings, metadata, and crawlable content. Ongoing keyword research, content publishing, and search performance work require a separate plan. Rankings are not guaranteed.

### Can you add booking or payment features?

Possibly. We’ll review the workflow, provider, technical requirements, and recurring costs before including an integration.

### What happens after launch?

The proposal defines the period for fixing issues within the delivered scope. Content changes, additional features, and continuing maintenance can be arranged separately.
