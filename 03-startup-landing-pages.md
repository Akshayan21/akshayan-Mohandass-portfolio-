You have a product to introduce and people you want to reach. The landing page needs to help them understand why it deserves their attention.

I design and build startup landing pages around the questions that come before a signup, demo request, or conversation.

## Turn the founder explanation into a page someone can follow.

You can explain the product because you know the problem, the decisions behind it, and what each feature is meant to do.

A visitor arrives without that context.

We’ll work out what they need to understand first, what evidence they need next, and what action makes sense for the product’s current stage.

That gives the page a direction before we decide how it looks.

## A landing page built around your next milestone

### Introduce an early product.

Help people understand the problem you’re addressing, who the product is for, and what exists today.

If the product is still in development, the copy will distinguish current functionality from planned work.

### Invite relevant demo requests.

Explain the use case and show enough of the product for a prospect to decide whether a conversation would be useful.

The enquiry flow can collect the details your team needs to prepare.

### Open a waitlist.

Give people a specific reason to register interest. Explain what they are joining and what happens after they submit their details.

The signup service, confirmation behaviour, and data destination are agreed before implementation.

### Replace a page that takes too much explaining.

If you keep sending follow-up messages to clarify the product, we can review what the page leaves unanswered.

The work may involve a new structure, clearer copy, more useful product visuals, or a simpler call to action.

## What landing page design and development can include

**Section ID:** included

### Message and page structure

We’ll define the audience, primary use case, and intended action. Then we’ll organise the page around them.

Depending on your product, that can include:

- A clear product introduction
- The problem and relevant use cases
- An explanation of how the product works
- Available product screenshots or demonstrations
- Verified customer evidence, where you have permission to use it
- Answers to common objections
- A consistent call to action

### Visual design

The design will give the message a clear reading order and show the product where explanation alone is insufficient.

We’ll use available brand assets and agree on any additional illustration or visual work. Conceptual visuals will be identifiable as concepts.

### Responsive development

The agreed design becomes a working page with:

- Layouts for relevant mobile and desktop screen sizes
- Defined button, form, and navigation behaviour
- An agreed signup, enquiry, or scheduling integration
- Descriptive titles, metadata, and page headings
- Launch preparation and handover

Analytics setup can be included when the platform, events, and access are agreed. Paid services and specialist integrations are scoped separately.

## What I’ll need from you

You don’t need a finished marketing brief. You do need a clear account of what the product does.

Useful starting material includes:

- The audience you want to reach
- The problem your product addresses
- Current functionality and release stage
- Screenshots, a prototype, or access to a demonstration
- The action you want visitors to take
- Any customer feedback or evidence you can publish
- Your launch timing and technical constraints

We’ll use this material to define the scope and identify what still needs to be produced.

## Questions about startup landing pages

### Can you work on the page before the product is finished?

Yes. The page can introduce an early product or collect interest. We’ll describe its current stage accurately and avoid presenting planned features as available functionality.

### Can you help with the copy?

Yes. Message structure and copy refinement can be included. More extensive positioning work, customer interviews, or original copywriting will be defined in the proposal.

### Can you connect my waitlist or CRM?

We can assess your chosen service and include a supported integration. Account access, subscription costs, and any custom development requirements need to be agreed first.

### Can you use my existing branding?

Yes. Share your brand guidelines and assets. If the visual identity is still developing, we can agree on a limited direction for the page without treating it as a full branding project.

### Will the page increase conversions?

A focused page can give visitors a clearer explanation and next step. Results also depend on your audience, traffic source, offer, and product. I do not promise a conversion uplift without evidence.

### Can we improve the page after launch?

Yes. Once you have useful feedback or analytics, we can scope changes around what the evidence suggests. Ongoing experimentation is a separate engagement.
